# Addendum to Section 1: Reference Sheet 
## Expanded Vocabulary: Feelings and Needs

Using a precise vocabulary allows you to pinpoint what is happening internally—*“I am feeling [Feeling] because I am needing [Need]”*—before deciding on a clear, actionable request.

### 1. Feelings When Needs Are Met

* **Affectionate & Warm:** Appreciative, compassionate, friendly, grateful, loving, open-hearted, sympathetic, tender, warm.
* **Peaceful & Grounded:** Calm, centered, comfortable, content, fulfilled, mellow, quiet, relaxed, relieved, secure, serene, steady, tranquil.
* **Joyful & Energized:** Amused, delighted, eager, ecstatic, elated, enthusiastic, excited, happy, hopeful, invigorated, joyful, optimistic, playful, thrilled.
* **Engaged & Curious:** Absorbed, alert, curious, engrossed, fascinated, inspired, interested, intrigued, involved, stimulated.
* **Confident & Empowered:** Assured, capable, clear-headed, confident, empowered, proud, resilient, strong.

### 2. Feelings When Needs Are Not Met

* **Afraid & Anxious:** Apprehensive, cautious, dread, fearful, guarded, insecure, jittery, nervous, overwhelmed, panicked, scared, uneasy, vulnerable, worried.
* **Angry & Frustrated:** Aggravated, annoyed, bitter, exasperated, frustrated, furious, impatient, irked, irritated, resentful.
* **Sad & Heavy:** Depressed, despondent, disappointed, discouraged, disheartened, gloomy, grief-stricken, heavy-hearted, helpless, hopeless, lonely, melancholy, sorrowful, unhappy.
* **Disconnected & Detached:** Alienated, aloof, apathetic, bored, detached, distant, flat, indifferent, numb, withdrawn.
* **Confused & Unsettled:** Ambivalent, baffled, bewildered, conflicted, disoriented, doubtful, hesitant, lost, perplexed, skeptical, torn, unsettled.
* **Vulnerable & Remorseful:** Ashamed, chagrined, embarrassed, flustered, guilty, mortified, regretful, remorseful, self-conscious.
* **Fatigued & Depleted:** Burned out, depleted, drained, exhausted, lethargic, listless, sleepy, tired, weary.
* **Tense & Agitated:** Agitated, cranky, distressed, edgy, fidgety, frazzled, irritable, restless, stressed, uptight.

### 3. Distinguishing True Feelings From "Faux Feelings"

Words that describe what you think someone else is doing *to* you (such as *"I feel ignored"* or *"I feel attacked"*) are evaluations disguised as feelings. Translating them into pure feelings and underlying needs keeps conversations open rather than triggering defensiveness.

| "Faux Feeling" (Implied Judgment) | Underlying True Feelings | Underlying Unmet Needs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Abandoned / Rejected** | Hurt, scared, lonely, sad | Connection, reassurance, belonging, inclusion |
| **Attacked / Criticized** | Scared, anxious, tense, hurt | Emotional safety, respect, understanding |
| **Dismissed / Invalidated** | Frustrated, sad, discouraged | Empathy, mutuality, to be heard and seen |
| **Ignored / Unimportant** | Lonely, sad, uneasy | Connection, consideration, mattering |
| **Manipulated / Pressured** | Resentful, frustrated, uneasy, guarded | Autonomy, choice, freedom, trust |
| **Misunderstood** | Frustrated, anxious, lonely | Clarity, understanding, empathy |
| **Taken for Granted** | Disappointed, tired, resentful | Appreciation, reciprocity, mutual care |
| **Unappreciated** | Sad, discouraged, heavy-hearted | Appreciation, acknowledgment, recognition |

### 4. Universal Human Needs

* **Connection & Belonging:** Acceptance, affection, appreciation, belonging, closeness, communication, community, companionship, compassion, consideration, consistency, empathy, inclusion, intimacy, love, mutuality, nurturing, respect, support, trust, understanding, warmth.
* **Autonomy & Agency:** Choice, freedom, independence, self-determination, space, spontaneity, sovereignty.
* **Safety & Stability:** Continuity, emotional safety, predictability, protection, reassurance, security, stability, steadiness.
* **Honesty & Integrity:** Authenticity, candor, clarity, congruence, honesty, integrity, self-responsibility, transparency.
* **Physical Well-Being:** Air, food, hydration, movement and exercise, physical comfort, rest and sleep, shelter, touch.
* **Peace & Ease:** Balance, beauty, calm, ease, harmony, order, peace, quiet, spaciousness.
* **Meaning & Growth:** Challenge, competence, contribution, creativity, discovery, growth, learning, purpose, self-expression.
* **Play & Joy:** Adventure, celebration, humor, joy, lightness, play, relaxation, stimulation.
